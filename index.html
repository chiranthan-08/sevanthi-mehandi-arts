Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

❦
How do I get the price for my bridal or event mehandi?

You can contact Sevanthi directly via WhatsApp (7406922333) or our inquiry form with your wedding/event date, venue, and desired hand coverage. Since all designs are personalized to your preferences, she will provide a custom quotation promptly!

Are all the photos shown on this website genuine work by Sevanthi?

Yes, 100%! Every single photograph displayed on this website showcases genuine client hands and feet handcrafted exclusively by Sevanthi. We never use artificial AI-generated henna imagery.

Are your henna cones organic and chemical-free?

Absolutely. Sevanthi uses only freshly mixed organic henna paste prepared with natural henna powder, eucalyptus oil, and sugar. It is completely safe for sensitive skin, mothers-to-be, and brides.

Do you travel to venues or out of Tumakuru for weddings?

Yes, Sevanthi travels across Tumakuru, surrounding taluks, and nearby regions for bridal appointments. Travel arrangements can be discussed during date inquiry.
